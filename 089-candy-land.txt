TITLE:
Candy Land — A Sweet Little World

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A sun-drenched confectionery valley rendered entirely in pastel: a candy-yellow sun glows over a gradient sky of blush pink melting into lavender, while three layers of rolling hills (lilac, mint, and fresh green) drift in a gentle horizontal parallax. A winding candy-cane path — pink-and-cream diagonal stripes drawn in an inline SVG pattern — threads from the foreground into the misty distance. Swaying lollipops on striped sticks bounce into place and rock on their stems, gumdrops with sugar-dusted tops dot the meadow, and a field of hand-drawn inline-SVG candies (rounds, wrapped swirls, domes) floats at varied depths so the whole world tilts and drifts as you move the pointer.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Layered SVG hill silhouettes with independent CSS translate parallax on pointer move.
- Inline SVG candy-cane path built from a rotated <pattern> of pink/cream stripes plus a shading gradient.
- CSS-drawn lollipops: gradient stick, radial-gradient swirl head, springy cubic-bezier "pop" entrance, and a pendulum sway keyframe with per-plant timing.
- Gumdrops assembled from a rounded-cone body, sugar-dot dot-grid overlay (radial-gradient background-image), and a soft highlight cap.
- JS-generated floating sweets (14) with randomized size, palette color, depth and z-index, driven by a smoothed requestAnimationFrame parallax loop.
- Subtle 3D scene tilt (perspective + rotateX/Y) for depth, plus drifting cloud puffs and twinkling sparkle stars.
- Fully responsive clamp() typography, italic serif display title, and prefers-reduced-motion fallback.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Passive exploration. Move the mouse (or drag on touch) to steer a soft parallax — hills, floating sweets and a gentle 3D tilt all respond at different depths, so the world feels alive and dimensional. The lollipops and gumdrops loop their own playful sway/bob regardless, rewarding a still moment with continuous gentle motion. No clicks required; it is a mood piece to drift through.
