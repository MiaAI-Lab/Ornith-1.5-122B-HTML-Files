The Silhouette of the Night

Silenced colour, amplified form. On the last runway of the season, ink black was not a shade but a stance — a single, unbroken line drawn from the brim of a hat to the hem of a train, cut only by a slash of vermilion. This is couture as restraint: the body remembered through shadow, the eye led by one deliberate, unblinking red.
