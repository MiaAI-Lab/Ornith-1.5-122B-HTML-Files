TITLE
=====
A WINTER IN GLASS — Interactive Snow Globe

DESIGN DESCRIPTION
==================
A hand-crafted snow globe centred on a full-canvas particle scene, framed by a
warm radial night-sky backdrop and a sculpted wooden base built from layered
CSS gradients (brass collar, banded wood, plinth). Inside the globe a soft
snow-lit diorama rises: a gradient sky, a distant pine-tree silhouette, a
chapel with a glowing window, a small snow-dusted mound, a lone tree and a
frozen pond, all drawn procedurally. A hundred snowflakes obey a real velocity
model — gravity, air resistance, a gentle ambient drift, and a circular
containment so snow naturally piles along the lower arc of the glass. When you
shake the globe, an "energy" value is injected: the flakes get a strong
tangential swirl plus an updraft and turbulence, then the energy decays and the
flakes settle back into the pile. Drifting glitter motes orbit the inner rim,
the glass dome is suggested by a refraction rim highlight, a soft top glow and
an inset vignette. Over it sits a serif italic gradient wordmark ("A Winter in
Glass") and a mono meta panel showing live energy, shake state and flake count.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES
=======================
- Velocity-based snow physics: per-flake gravity + air resistance (exponential
  damping), a sinusoidal ambient drift, and circular containment that reflects
  velocity off the inner glass so snow accumulates along the bottom arc.
- Shake energy model: pointer drag injects energy proportional to pointer
  speed; energy drives a tangential swirl force, an updraft and turbulence,
  then decays exponentially toward rest — the swirl-then-settle signature.
- Refill trickle: flakes resting slowly in the deep pile occasionally lift back
  to the top so a calm globe never fully freezes into a static pile.
- Procedural diorama on a clip circle: gradient sky, pine silhouette, chapel
  with a lit window, snow mound, lone tree, frozen pond — all canvas, all
  redrawn behind the falling snow.
- Glass effect: a refraction rim (soft inner highlight), a top arc sheen, a
  soft outer glow and an inset vignette sell the sphere without any image.
- Glitter motes orbiting the inner rim with per-mote twinkle phase; additive
  glow on the snow-lit sky and chapel window.
- DPR-aware canvas, responsive re-layout on resize, live energy readout.
- Editorial serif italic gradient wordmark + mono meta panel.
- prefers-reduced-motion freezes the loop; touch support included.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL
==========================
Tactile interactive object. Move the pointer over the globe and drag — the
faster you flick, the more "energy" you pour in, and the snow roils: it swirls
around the globe in a vortex, lifts on an updraft and scatters with turbulence.
Release and the energy bleeds away through air resistance; the flakes slow,
drift down and pile gently along the bottom of the glass, exactly like a real
snow globe. Clicking/tapping gives a quick jolt of the same swirl. A live
"energy" bar and shake-state readout track the disturbance. A custom glassy
cursor follows fine-pointing devices and hides on touch. Fully responsive,
offline, and motion-sensitive friendly (animation halts under
prefers-reduced-motion).
