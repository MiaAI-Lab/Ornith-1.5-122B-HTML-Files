TITLE: Water Ripple — Disturb the Surface

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A full-bleed expanse of clear blue water rendered as a real-time height-field simulation on a single canvas. The surface is never still: a slow ambient swell rolls across it, and wherever the pointer touches or drags, concentric ripples are injected, propagate outward, reflect softly and diffuse as energy spreads and decays. The shading reads as genuine liquid — deep cerulean troughs, bright cyan crests, and shifting caustic highlights that gather where the simulated waves focus, all blended to feel reflective and wet rather than a flat gradient.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Classic two-buffer height-field water simulation (current + previous height grids) with diffusion/decay each frame
- Pointer-driven splash injection (down = single drop, drag = continuous wake) mapped to the grid
- Caustic color shading: height + velocity used to tint crests bright and draw focusing highlight streaks
- Ambient base swell so the surface animates without input
- Subtle surface reflection via gradient + specular term on the height gradient
- Responsive canvas sizing with devicePixelRatio handling; reduced-motion lowers resolution / slows decay

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Direct, tactile manipulation. Click or tap to drop a stone and watch rings expand; press-drag to carve a wake that follows the cursor. Multiple disturbances overlap and interfere naturally. It also runs on its own (ambient swell) for passive viewing, and stops responding to input gracefully when off-canvas. Fully offline, no dependencies; reduced-motion keeps it calm.
