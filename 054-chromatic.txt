TITLE:

"Chromatic" — bold type fractured into displaced red, green and blue channels.


DESIGN DESCRIPTION:

On a pure black stage, oversized editorial type ("CHROMATIC" / "ABERRATION") is split into three color copies — red, green and blue — each offset along its own animated vector so the channels separate and recombine like a failing lens. A crisp white core sits beneath the colored ghosts, and thin prismatic bars, scanlines and drifting RGB noise add glitch texture. Floating geometric shapes (a ring, a triangle, a square) repeat the same RGB-split treatment and drift through the composition. The whole piece is a high-contrast, motion-driven study of optical dispersion: stark, technical and cold, punctuated by the spectral colors.


MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:

- Multi-layer text with three color channels (rgba red/green/blue) plus a white core, each channel offset by an animated, per-channel vector to create the chromatic split.
- Canvas RGB-noise / static field rendered additively over the black background for a filmic glitch texture, updated each frame.
- CSS-driven floating shapes (ring, triangle, square) with their own channel displacement and drift keyframes.
- Pointer-driven displacement: mouse X/Y drives the separation amount and direction of the channels, so the aberration reacts live.
- Scanline + prismatic bar overlays and a subtle vignette for a screen/glitch atmosphere.
- System serif + mono type pairing, all-lowercase metadata labels, and a crosshair cursor target for a technical UI feel.
- Responsive (clamp/vw/vh, resize) 320px->4K; prefers-reduced-motion freezes the animation and pointer motion.


INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:

- Move the pointer across the page to push the RGB channels apart and together — horizontal and vertical movement steer the displacement direction and magnitude.
- The type and shapes continuously drift on their own animated channel offsets even at rest, so the piece is always subtly alive.
- Click / tap to trigger a one-shot "glitch" burst (a sudden large channel separation and noise spike) that settles back to the idle drift.
- No scrolling or navigation: a single full-bleed art canvas built to be explored through the cursor.
