TITLE
-----
Misty Mountains — Layered Ridges

DESIGN DESCRIPTION
------------------
A serene, pale luminous sky fades from cool blue-grey at the top to a faint warm haze at the horizon, where a soft sun glows and slowly breathes. Below it, four mountain ridges are stacked as inline SVG silhouettes, each a step deeper and more saturated blue-grey than the one behind it — atmospheric perspective in pure vector — and each drifting horizontally at its own speed so nearer ranges slide further than distant ones, giving a living parallax. Bands of heavily-blurred fog weave between and over the ranges, moving counter to the ridges, while a small flock of simple stroked birds circles high in the sky, bobbing gently. The palette is entirely soft blue-grey with a whisper of warmth, and the composition is quiet and horizontal, evoking a still alpine morning.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES
-----------------------
- Stacked inline SVG ridge layers: four hand-drawn <path> silhouettes with jagged peaks, stretched via viewBox + preserveAspectRatio="none", filled in a graded blue-grey ramp (far #c9d2dc → near #64737f) for atmospheric perspective — no images.
- Parallax drift: each ridge layer oscillates horizontally at a different duration/amplitude (nearer = faster, larger), so the ranges shift relative to one another like real depth.
- Animated fog bands: full-width divs with a soft top-bottom fade and heavy filter:blur(20px), drifting on their own slow loops, some weaving between the mountain layers for depth.
- Gentle sun glow: a large radial-gradient disc with a blurred bright core, slowly pulsing opacity/scale (sunPulse) for a luminous, breathing light.
- Birds: minimal double-arc stroked paths in four small flock members, each with a staggered vertical bob plus a slow horizontal drift.
- Fully responsive fixed inset:0 scene with vmin/vh units scaling 320px to 4K; complete prefers-reduced-motion fallback that freezes all motion and holds the parallax at a resting offset.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL
--------------------------
Purely contemplative and ambient — there is no click target. The viewer simply watches the scene breathe: ridges drift, fog weaves, the sun pulses and birds bob in an endless, calm loop. Motion is deliberately slow and non-distracting. When reduced motion is requested, everything holds still at a composed resting frame. No navigation — the frame is the landscape.
