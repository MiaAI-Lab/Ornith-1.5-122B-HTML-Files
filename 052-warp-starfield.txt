TITLE:
"Warp — Hyperspace Transit"

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A cockpit view of a starship tearing through hyperspace. Stars live in true pseudo-3D space: each has an (x, y, z) position and is projected from a central vanishing point, so as it rushes toward the camera it accelerates outward and stretches into a radial streak — the classic warp effect. The field is additive-glowing white and pale-blue streaks fanning from a luminous central core that brightens with speed. Behind it, a static deep-space backdrop of nebula clouds and far dim stars smears and blue-shifts as velocity climbs. A minimal sci-fi HUD frames the view — corner brackets, a rotating reticle, live warp-factor and distance readouts, and a scanline overlay — all rendered in cool ice-blue on near-black.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Pseudo-3D star projection: screen = center + (x, y) * (focal / z), with z decreasing each frame so stars accelerate toward the viewer.
- Streaking: each star is drawn as a line from its position at z to its position at z + streak, where streak length scales with speed, producing radial warp lines that lengthen near the center.
- Additive ("lighter") compositing and per-star brightness that ramps up as z shrinks, plus a bright head dot on each streak.
- A central hyperspace core glow (radial gradient) that intensifies with speed, and a background canvas that smears via CSS scale + blur tied to velocity.
- Parallax steering: pointer / touch shifts the vanishing point and drifts the nebula backdrop.
- Sci-fi HUD chrome in pure CSS (corner brackets, animated rotating reticle, scanlines, tabular warp readouts) for a premium cockpit feel.
- Fully responsive full-viewport canvas with devicePixelRatio-aware sizing.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
A speed-first flight deck. The "Warp" slider (0–10) sets cruise velocity; the field stretches and the HUD updates live. A big "Engage Hyperspace" button triggers a ramp-up to warp 10 with a blue flash and a brief hold, then eases back to cruise. Moving the pointer / touching the screen steers the tunnel (parallax). Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion by holding a calm, slow drift.
