DESIGN DESCRIPTION
A pressure plant behind a riveted brass plate. Five gears of differing tooth-counts form two meshing chains across the panel, each wheel drawn with a real trapezoidal tooth profile, a radial brass gradient, spokes, a hub and a glowing axle, and every gear turning at a ratio inversely proportional to its tooth count so the trains mesh believably. A large pressure gauge sits top-right with a cream face, tick marks, colored danger zones and a red needle that trembles with the boiler pressure; below it a brass PSI readout ticks up and down. Riveted tubes run through the background, a warm radial glow pools behind the train, and periodic puffs of soft steam rise from a vent. A riveted plate frame with domed rivets around all four edges and an italic-serif editorial overlay complete the look.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES
- Meshing gears drawn on canvas with a true tooth profile (root→tip→root per tooth), radial brass/copper gradients, spokes, hubs and axle holes.
- Meshing ratios: each gear's angular speed is set to base × (driverTeeth / itsTeeth) with alternating direction, so the chain turns at believable relative rates.
- A detailed pressure gauge: radial plate, cream face gradient, tick marks with danger coloring, a colored danger arc, printed numerals and a red needle driven by an oscillating pressure value.
- Periodic steam puffs on a separate canvas — soft radial-gradient blobs that rise, expand, fade and recycle on a timer.
- A riveted plate frame built from CSS: a rounded inset plate with layered box-shadows plus DOM rivets placed around all four edges.
- Background riveted pipes with a brass linear gradient for depth.
- Brass/copper/sepia palette with a warm ambient glow and tabular-nums PSI readout; click to release a burst of steam.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL
Passive: the gear trains rotate continuously at their meshing ratios, the needle trembles as boiler pressure oscillates, and the boiler exhales a soft steam puff at intervals. Click anywhere to release a burst of steam at the center. Everything slows but keeps running under prefers-reduced-motion.
