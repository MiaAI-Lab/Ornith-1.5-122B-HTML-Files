TITLE:
LUMINA — Painting with Living Light

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
Against a pure-black void, autonomous "painters" sweep glowing trails across the frame, each following an invisible flow field so their paths curl into organic, calligraphic loops. Every stroke is laid down as an additive, layered bloom — a hot near-white core wrapped in concentric cyan, magenta or gold halos — and the canvas is never fully cleared: a thin black veil is dropped each frame so the light lingers as a phosphor afterglow and slowly decays, exactly like a long photographic exposure. A soft radial vignette, a faint screen-blended grain, a serif wordmark, a live "strokes on screen" counter and a three-swatches colour legend frame the piece as a darkroom instrument for sculpting photons.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Long-exposure trail effect via a per-frame semi-transparent black fill (rgba(0,0,0,~0.028)) instead of clearRect, so strokes persist and decay like afterglow phosphor.
- Additive bloom helper that draws each light point as a radial gradient (bright core → mid halo → transparent) with a separate near-white hot core, composited with globalCompositeOperation:"lighter" so overlapping trails flare white-hot.
- Six autonomous painters steered by a smooth sine-based pseudo-noise flow field, each assigned a cyan/magenta/gold family and a per-stroke phase so no two trails look alike.
- Fan of offset dots behind each painter's head to give the strokes real width, wobble and taper rather than a thin line.
- Pointer light that renders a colour-cycling, velocity-thickened tail (parabola-faded segments) plus speed-spawned sparkles, so fast movement throws off sparks.
- DPR-aware canvas (capped at 2×) for crisp glow on retina/4K, delta-time loop, edge-bounce containment, a live stroke counter and prefers-reduced-motion that thins the painter count and slows the decay.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Pointer / touch driven, no buttons. Moving the cursor paints your own glowing light stroke — it thickens and brightens with speed and throws off sparks on fast sweeps; holding the button intensifies the glow. Autonomous painters continuously paint in the background so the canvas is never static, and every mark slowly fades back to black. A live counter in the legend tracks how many strokes are currently burning on screen. prefers-reduced-motion keeps the scene present but thins it to a few slow painters with a faster, calmer decay.
