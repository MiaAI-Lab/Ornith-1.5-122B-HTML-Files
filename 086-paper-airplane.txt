TITLE: Paper Airplane — A Loop Through the Clouds

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A crisp folded paper airplane glides along a gentle looping arc through a bright, layered sky. The palette is warm and airy — gradient sky blue deepening upward, a soft golden sun low on the horizon, and billowing white clouds rendered purely in CSS gradients. Multiple cloud rows drift past at different speeds to create real parallax depth, while a few paper leaves and a faint contrail trail the plane. The plane itself is built from angled polygon facets with crisp crease highlights and soft folded-paper drop shadows, giving it tangible thickness as it banks along its path.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Paper plane assembled from CSS polygon facets (polygons/clips) with crease highlights and layered drop shadows for folded-paper thickness
- Multi-layer parallax: several cloud rows (pure CSS radial/elliptical gradients) translating at staggered speeds
- A looping flight path driven by CSS keyframes (translate + rotate) so the plane banks and arcs naturally
- Warm sun glow via stacked radial gradients and soft blur
- Drifting leaves / paper bits with sway-and-rotate keyframes for ambient life
- Gentle bob and contrail fade for delight without clutter
- Georgia/serif display headline with airy letter-spacing; reduced-motion freezes drift and flight

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Largely ambient and cinematic — it loops forever as a living wallpaper. On pointer down / tap, the plane performs a quick barrel-roll flourish and a fresh gust of clouds is released; moving the pointer left/right nudges the parallax speed so the viewer feels a light breeze. No scrolling or complex input required; fully self-playing with a reduced-motion fallback.
