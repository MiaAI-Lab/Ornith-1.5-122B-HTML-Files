TITLE:
Zentangle — "Ink Meditation, Patterns That Draw Themselves"

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A meditative ink study on warm, faintly graduated paper. The page is tiled edge-to-edge with square cells, each holding a different ornamental motif drawn in black ink — mandala petals, concentric rings, Archimedean spirals, crosshatch, fish scales, stippled dots, sine waves, layered rosettes and nested chevrons. On load (and every regenerate) the whole composition "grows": each tile first sketches its inked border, then its pattern draws itself on with a slow, eased reveal — petals unfurl, spirals unspool, scales stack row by row — staggered as a gentle wave sweeping across the grid so the eye follows the growth. The palette is strictly black ink on warm white, echoing sumi-e / pen-and-ink practice, with a quiet uppercase label and a single "Regenerate" button. The result is hypnotic and calming: a pattern that never sits still as it settles into place.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- "Grow" reveal animation: each motif is drawn as a sequence of elements and rendered only up to an eased progress value (0→1 over ~4.4s), so patterns draw themselves on; fractional progress draws a partially-grown last element.
- Seamless tiling: a full-viewport grid of tiles, each assigned a random motif, seed and rotation, filling 320px→4K responsively.
- Deterministic per-tile RNG (seeded) so each cell's jitter/variation is stable across frames but unique.
- Nine hand-built ink motifs (petal/vesica curves, spirals, scales, waves, chevrons, stippling, crosshatch, rosette, rings) rendered on a single <canvas> with round caps/joins for a pen-like stroke.
- Animated tile frames that sketch their four edges in sequence before the interior pattern fills, reinforcing the "being drawn" feel.
- Staggered start times (by col+row) so the growth ripples across the composition; DPR-aware crisp rendering.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Meditative and self-running — the pattern grows into place on its own, slowly and continuously. Two deliberate interactions: tap any single tile to redraw it (it re-rolls its motif and animates its own growth immediately), or press "Regenerate" to reshuffle the entire composition and watch a fresh pattern grow across the page. No timers, scores or modal states — a calm, endless drawing surface.
