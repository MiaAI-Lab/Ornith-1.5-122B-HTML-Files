PROMPT (exact, as issued):
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Build a standalone, self-contained HTML page: a city at night with
randomly twinkling windows, neon signs and rain-slick reflections.
Palette: midnight blue, warm window glow, neon pink/cyan. Signature: CSS
skyline silhouette, randomized window twinkle, neon sign glow flicker,
wet-street reflection plane.
Constraints: one self-contained .html, ALL CSS in one <style> and ALL JS
in one <script> (inline everything), ZERO external dependencies (no
CDN, no external fonts — system stacks only, no external images/SVG
files/libraries), works fully offline by double-clicking with no network
requests, responsive 320px->4K, valid HTML5, Awwwards-caliber original
art direction.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
TITLE:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
MIDNIGHT · City of Wet Light

------------------------------------------------------------------------
DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
After the rain, a midnight-blue skyline comes alive: a procedurally
generated silhouette of towers holds thousands of warm windows that blink
on and off at random, while neon signs in pink, cyan and amber hum and
stutter above the avenue. Every colour dissolves into the wet street
below, where a flipped, blurred reflection ripples and shimmers — the
avenue has turned to a mirror of light.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Procedurally generated SVG skyline silhouette (random building widths,
  heights, rooftop ledges and antennas) for a unique city each load.
- Thousands of SVG windows driven by a JS twinkle loop that randomly
  flicks opacity up and down, with occasional full on/off cutoffs.
- CSS neon signs using layered text-shadow glows and stepped flicker
  keyframes (pink / cyan / amber) for authentic stutter.
- A wet-street reflection plane: a cloned skyline flipped with
  transform:scaleY(-1), blurred, dimmed and masked into the pavement.
- Canvas rain streaks (screen blend) plus a canvas ripple layer that
  draws expanding elliptical puddle rings on pointer movement.
- Twinkling starfield canvas, city-glow gradient, fog, sheen and a heavy
  vignette to seat the composition in night.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Atmospheric and self-running — windows twinkle, neon flickers, rain falls
and reflections shimmer on their own. The pointer acts as a stone in a
puddle: moving it draws ripples across the wet-street reflection, and a
click (or the occasional automatic trigger) fires a brief lightning flash
that washes the whole city in cold light. Interaction enhances the mood
without disrupting it.
