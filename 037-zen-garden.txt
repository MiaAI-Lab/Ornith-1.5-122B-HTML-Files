TITLE
-----
Still Water, Raked Sand — An Interactive Zen Garden

DESIGN DESCRIPTION
------------------
A minimalist composition sits on a soft green moss field: a bed of warm sand raked into concentric circles, three smooth grey stones arranged in an asymmetric triad (the classic "sanzui" grouping), and a single fallen leaf. The whole garden is rendered on a canvas whose sand grain is drawn procedurally, so the surface has a fine, tactile speckle rather than a flat fill. Dragging across the sand sweeps a rake through it, carving clean parallel grooves that part the grain and reveal a slightly darker layer beneath; the rake leaves a gentle ripple at its edges. Motion is deliberately slow and sparse — a drifting leaf, a faint breathing highlight across the water's edge — so the scene reads as calm and meditative rather than busy.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES
-----------------------
- Procedural canvas sand texture: layered seeded noise + fine grain speckle, no image assets.
- Drag-rake trails: a moving "rake" mask carves parallel grooves by clearing/re-painting grain along the drag path, revealing a darker sub-layer.
- Concentric raked rings around each stone, drawn as curved grooves to echo real zen-garden motifs.
- Stones built from radial gradients for a rounded, wet-stone volume with soft cast shadows.
- A single canvas loop compositing base sand, raked marks, stones, moss border and a drifting leaf — all offline, all generated.
- Muted, curated palette (warm sand #d9c7a3, grey stones, soft green moss) and generous negative space for a calm, editorial feel.
- Reduced-motion fallback: animation pauses, rake still works.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL
--------------------------
Pointer / touch drag across the garden rakes the sand, leaving trailing grooves that follow the gesture; lifting the pointer ends the stroke. On touch devices the garden is pan-dragged with a single finger and raked at the same time. A short hint ("drag to rake") appears once and fades. No buttons or menus — the entire surface is the instrument, reinforcing the meditative, tool-less experience.
