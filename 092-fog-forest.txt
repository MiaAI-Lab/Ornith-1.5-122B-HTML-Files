TITLE:
Fog Forest — God Rays

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A mysterious forest shrouded in drifting mist, lit from above by volumetric god rays. Four receding layers of hand-drawn pine silhouettes sit in atmospheric perspective — pale grey-green in the distance, near-black green in the foreground — each drifting and parallaxing at its own depth so the scene has real spatial recession. Soft blurred fog bands sweep horizontally at different tempos, while pale dust motes float through the light. Volumetric light shafts radiate from a pale sun source and sway gently, rendered with additive blending over a muted green/grey sky. A heavy vignette pulls the eye inward and deepens the moody, luminous stillness.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Four procedurally generated pine-silhouette layers (deterministic pseudo-random) with depth-scaled parallax and slow wind drift
- Volumetric god rays drawn as additive (lighter) gradient triangles fanning from a swaying sun source, plus a soft radial sun glow
- Layered CSS fog bands with heavy blur, each on its own slow drift animation
- Atmospheric perspective via a light-to-dark muted green/grey palette across depth layers
- Drifting dust motes with additive blending and gentle sinusoidal sway
- Moody radial vignette and a serif, wide-tracked title treatment
- DPR-aware canvas resize; pointer-driven parallax; reduced-motion fallback

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Slow and ambient — mist drifts and trees sway continuously. Moving the pointer (or finger) shifts the tree layers in parallax, giving the sense of drifting sideways through the forest, while the god rays breathe and sway on their own. Motion is gracefully reduced under prefers-reduced-motion.
