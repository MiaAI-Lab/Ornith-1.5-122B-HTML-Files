TITLE
-----
Twilight Ridge — a paper-cut diorama that breathes as you move.


DESIGN DESCRIPTION
------------------
A dusk scene built like stacked sheets of cut card. A warm-to-cool sky gradient
runs from deep teal at the zenith down through sage to a cream horizon, where a
soft golden sun glows and slowly pulses. Five receding mountain ridges — each a
hand-shaped SVG silhouette in progressively deeper teals — sit one behind the
other, drifting sideways at their own pace so nearer ranges race past farther
ones. Wispy fog bands weave between the ridges, and a small flock of hand-drawn
birds drifts across the sky, wrapping gently. Everything reacts to the pointer:
moving or dragging the mouse slides the layers at different depths, giving a true
sense of looking through a cut-paper window. A subtle vignette, faint film grain
and twinkling star-dust near the top complete the mood, while a serif caption
anchors the piece.


MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES
-----------------------
- Stacked SVG ridge silhouettes generated procedurally from layered sine waves
  (two harmonics + a detail term) for organic, non-repeating mountain profiles.
- Seamless auto parallax: each ridge is drawn twice across a 2×-width SVG and the
  group is translated by (time × speed) modulo one tile width, so the drift loops
  perfectly with no visible jump.
- Depth-scaled mouse parallax: pointer position (-1..1) offsets every layer by a
  fraction of its depth, with near ranges moving more than far ones for real
  three-dimensional feel; pointer motion is eased (lerped) for smoothness.
- Distinct per-layer speed/depth/colour/height/phase values create convincing
  atmospheric perspective (lighter, slower, hazier in the distance).
- Blurred radial-gradient fog bands and a screen-ish vignette + dotted grain
  overlay add atmosphere and paper-cut texture without any external assets.
- A glowing radial-gradient sun with dual drop-shadow bloom that shifts slightly
  with the pointer and breathes over time.
- Procedural SVG flock of birds that traverse and wrap the sky with a soft bob.
- devicePixelRatio-independent vector SVG that rebuilds its paths on resize/
  orientation, keeping the ridgelines crisp from 320px to 4K.


INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL
--------------------------
- Move the mouse (or drag on touch) anywhere to look left/right through the
  scene; each layer responds at its own depth for a parallax "peek" effect.
- The landscape also drifts continuously on its own (auto parallax), so it feels
  alive even with no input, and a tap nudges the viewpoint.
- A caption invites interaction; the hint fades out on the first move.
- Fully responsive: all geometry (ridge widths, bird spacing, tile widths) is
  recomputed from the live viewport on resize and orientation change, and layers
  are rebuilt so nothing distorts or clips at any width.
- No controls or menus — the interaction is purely embodied (look through the
  window), keeping the composition clean and gallery-like.
- Runs entirely offline with zero network requests; every visual is CSS/SVG/JS.
