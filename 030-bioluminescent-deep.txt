TITLE:
ABYSSAL — Living Light in the Dark

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
Far below the photic zone, a black-water canvas is alive with drifting bioluminescence. Dozens of motes — from faint pinpricks to large soft bokeh — pulse with cyan-green light at their own rhythms, each drawn as an additive radial gradient so overlapping glows bloom white-hot where they meet. A slow buoyant drift and gentle sine sway carry them upward and sideways; occasionally a mote flares on its own, and when the water is disturbed the nearby organisms ignite in a brightening cascade. A layered radial "deep" gradient, a serif wordmark, a live depth readout in metres, and a quiet caption frame the scene as an instrument panel lowered into the abyss.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Full-bleed <canvas> with devicePixelRatio scaling (capped at 2×) and a transform-based resize that keeps the glow crisp on retina and 4K displays.
- Per-particle additive rendering (globalCompositeOperation:"lighter") with radial gradients from a cyan-green HSLA core to fully transparent, producing true optical bloom.
- Independent per-mote pulse phase, drift velocity, sway amplitude and hue (150–198°) so the field never looks uniform; larger "bokeh" motes add depth parallax.
- Transient flash state on each particle (random spontaneous flares + triggered flares) that inflates the glow radius for a realistic organism ignition, then decays.
- Expanding radial-gradient ripple rings on click/tap to sell the "disturbed water" bioluminescent reaction.
- requestAnimationFrame loop with delta-time, buoyant wrap-around, a live depth counter eased toward a scroll target, and prefers-reduced-motion support that slows motion.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Pointer / touch / scroll driven, no buttons. Moving the cursor stirs the water: nearby motes are gently pushed away and brighten, as if displaced by a current. Clicking or tapping triggers an expanding flash ring and ignites the organisms within range — a bioluminescent bloom. Scrolling (or trackpad swipe) descends or ascends through the water column, easing a live "Depth" readout in metres. Motes flare spontaneously at random even when untouched, so the abyss is always quietly breathing. prefers-reduced-motion keeps the scene present but slows it to a near-still drift.
