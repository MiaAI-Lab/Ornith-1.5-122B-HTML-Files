TITLE:
Lightning Storm — "Tempest №098"

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A brooding, cinematic night sky built from layered gradients — deep storm grey at the zenith melting into a warmer electric glow along the horizon. Heavy cloud banks drift slowly across the canvas, and behind them the air itself seems charged. The signature bolts are true canvas lightning: each strike is generated with a midpoint-displacement (fractal subdivision) algorithm that splits a single line into a jagged spine, then recursively throws thinner, dimmer branches off the main channel in white-hot and violet hues. When a bolt lands, the whole screen flashes a bright radial overlay that decays in a fraction of a second, the horizon flares, and a subtle screen shake ripples the composition — the visual echo of a distant rumble. Quiet uppercase typography and a thin "tap to summon a strike" tag keep the frame editorial rather than gamey.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Midpoint-displacement lightning generation: a straight segment is recursively displaced at its midpoint (with a turbulence/jitter term) to produce natural jagged bolts.
- Recursive branching: the main spine spawns thinner, lower-alpha child bolts at random points, each with its own displacement seed.
- Multi-pass glow: each bolt is stroked several times with increasing width and decreasing alpha (violet halo → pale core → white-hot center) for a luminous, electric bloom.
- Full-screen flash overlay: a radial-gradient layer whose opacity spikes and decays per strike, simulating ambient light scatter.
- Subtle transform-based screen shake triggered on impact for a tactile "rumble" feel.
- Slowly parallaxed cloud bands via layered radial/linear gradients animated with requestAnimationFrame.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Ambient storm: bolts occasionally strike on their own at random intervals across the sky. The primary interaction is deliberate — click or tap anywhere to summon a lightning strike aimed at that point, complete with its own flash and shake. No menus or modes; the sky is a live, clickable canvas.
