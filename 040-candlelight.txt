TITLE

A Small Warmth — Candlelight & Quiet Verse

DESIGN DESCRIPTION
------------------
A single candle glows in an otherwise near-black room, its amber light spilling out in a soft vignette that is the only illumination. The flame is pure CSS — a layered teardrop of radial gradients (hot white core, orange body, cool blue base) that flickers and sways on a fast stepped animation, while a large halo behind it pulses gently in and out as if breathing. Dozens of tiny ember particles drift up from the flame tip on canvas, glowing hot yellow before cooling to deep orange and fading. Beside the candle, a few lines of cozy italic poetry sit in warm cream type, kept intimate and small so the whole scene feels like a quiet, candlelit moment.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES
-----------------------
- CSS-only flame: a teardrop shape from asymmetric border-radius, built in three gradient layers (body, bright inner core, blue base) with a fast stepped flicker + sway keyframe.
- Soft glow pulse: a large blurred radial halo behind the flame animates scale and opacity to mimic the flame's breathing light.
- Canvas ember particles: warm dots spawn at the flame tip, rise with a gentle swirl, cool from yellow to orange, and fade via a sine life-curve.
- Warm vignette: a radial gradient lit only around the candle so the edges fall to near-black, selling the "one light in a dark room" mood.
- Candle body with melt pool, wax drips and a brass dish, all built from layered gradients and box-shadows — no images.
- Cozy editorial typography: italic serif verse in warm cream with a restrained amber eyebrow and signature line.
- Reduced-motion fallback: flicker and pulse freeze to a steady, calm flame.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL
--------------------------
Atmospheric and hands-off — the candle simply burns, the flame flickers, the glow breathes and embers rise on their own, meant to be left glowing in the background. There are no controls or navigation; the piece is a meditative still-life to sit with, pairing the living flame with the quiet poem.
