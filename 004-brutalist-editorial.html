The beauty of a hard edge.

Words · J. Marchetti18 min

In an age of soft gradients and rounded corners, a quiet rebellion is reclaiming the right angle. Brutalism on the web is not laziness — it is a refusal to pretend the medium is anything other than structure, ink and grid.

The best editorial layouts treat type as architecture: heavy serifs stacked against monospace, borders that do not hide, shadows that refuse to blur. Nothing apologizes. Everything is on the record.
