TITLE:
Threads of Liquid Light — Jewel-Toned Silk Ribbons

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
Five luminous silk ribbons drift through a deep, near-black void, each one a continuous sum of three sine waves so the cloth folds and rolls rather than merely waving. Every ribbon carries its own jewel palette — emerald, sapphire, ruby, amethyst, gold — shaded from a deep edge tone through a saturated mid to a bright specular core. A per-ribbon twist term computes how much the face catches the light, driving both a symmetric glossy gradient across the width and the intensity of a bright specular spine down the centre. Soft ambient radial glows "breathe" behind the cloth, floating light motes pass in front of and behind the strands for depth, and a vignette focuses the eye. Editorial serif display type (Georgia) with a gradient-clipped accent word and tabular "Warp/Weft" readouts sit quietly over the motion.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas ribbons generated as a sum of three sine waves per strand (position-based, not sprite-based).
- Per-sample normal + twist term to build left/right ribbon edges and a glossy symmetric gradient (createLinearGradient along the ribbon width).
- Facing/ specular calculation (facingSum) to modulate a bright core stroke — the "silk catching light" effect.
- Pointer parallax with eased smoothing; motes split into far/ front layers for true depth ordering.
- Scroll-driven phase offset ("change the weave") and an animated tabular Warp counter.
- Ambient radial-gradient glow layer with mix-blend-mode:screen and a CSS vignette.
- requestAnimationFrame with delta-time and prefers-reduced-motion throttling.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Passive cinematic drift by default. Moving the pointer parts the cloth via parallax (nearer ribbons and motes lead the farther ones); scrolling shifts the weave phase. prefers-reduced-motion slows the animation substantially and stops the ambient breathing. Canvas redraws on resize with a capped DPR for crispness on 4K. Fully responsive.
