An abstract quantum probability-wave grid, a warped 3D-ish lattice of glowing

TITLE
=====
QUANTUM · Probability Field

DESIGN DESCRIPTION
==================
A deep-space data-vision piece. A large lattice of glowing nodes is rendered as
a warped plane tilted toward the viewer in perspective, its surface displaced
up and down by a live "wavefunction" — the probability amplitude of each node.
The amplitude field is a sum of travelling planar waves, a concentric outburst
ripple and one bright wavepacket that sweeps diagonally across the grid, so the
lattice continuously breathes and ripples. Lines connecting neighbouring nodes
and the nodes themselves are coloured along an electric-blue → violet → magenta
gradient driven by their amplitude, drawn with additive (lighter) blending so
intersections bloom like excited quantum states. A field of faint drifting
motes and a star-dust layer float behind, while a vignette and inset haze give
it a monitor-in-a-dark-room feel. Over this sits a restrained editorial HUD: a
gradient wordmark, a mono readout panel (state, entangled-node count, live
amplitude, wavepacket phase), a probability legend bar and an interaction hint.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES
=======================
- 3D lattice projected with true perspective: per-node rotate-around-X (tilt)
  and rotate-around-Y (mouse steer), then focal/(focal+z+cam) perspective
  divide; perspective scale also drives node size/brightness (depth cue).
- Amplitude field = interference of planar sine waves + concentric ripple ×
  exponential falloff + a Gaussian wavepacket that travels across the lattice.
- Node world-height y = amplitude × k, so the probability wave is a visible 3D
  surface, not just a colour map.
- Additive blending (globalCompositeOperation:'lighter') for lines and nodes;
  radial-gradient haloes per node for the glowing intersections.
- Luminous trail persistence: each frame fades the previous with a translucent
  deep-space fill instead of a hard clear.
- Drifting motes + a radial-gradient star-dust ::before layer, twinkle via a
  sine of a phase accumulator.
- Click "collapse" fires an expanding ripple (ring radius grows, amplitude
  decays) plus a radial burst of motes from the pointer.
- Cursor drives yaw/pitch targets with eased easing for the "warp the
  viewpoint" signature; DPR-aware canvas scaling; responsive focal length.
- Editorial HUD with gradient background-clip text, mono type, pulsing status
  dot, and a probability legend gradient bar.
- prefers-reduced-motion freezes all integration/animation and kills the dot
  pulse; small screens drop the side panels.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL
==========================
Ambient-first with light pointer interaction. The wavefield, motes and ripples
animate automatically and loop forever. Moving the cursor warps the viewpoint
(eased yaw + pitch tilt of the whole lattice). Clicking / tapping "collapses" a
probability burst: an expanding amplitude ring radiates from the field and a
spray of glowing motes erupts from the pointer. Everything is pointer/touch and
resize friendly, runs offline, and degrades to a static-but-still-animated (or
fully still under prefers-reduced-motion) experience on small screens and for
motion-sensitive users.
