The Wreck · No. 070

Where the light
reaches the silent hull.

Resting on the silt for a century, the hull still catches the shafts that fall from the surface. Marine snow drifts through the teal gloom; kelp sways in a tide with no wind; and now and then a silver school sweeps across the ruin like a thought.
