TITLE:

"Lantern Festival" — a thousand warm lights rising into an indigo night.


DESIGN DESCRIPTION:

A deep-indigo night sky, dusted with twinkling gold and white stars (some with delicate sparkle crosses), fills the frame while a soft moon glows in the upper corner. Hand-drawn paper lanterns — warm orange ovals with ribs, caps, a hanging thread and a large additive glow halo — rise continuously from below the horizon, each swaying on its own sine path and drifting at its own speed. Beneath them, a silhouetted hillside and town rooftop line sit above a band of dark water, where every lantern casts a shimmering warm reflection made of a soft vertical streak plus wobbling highlight dashes. Slow ember sparks float up from the horizon glow, and the entire scene gently parallaxes with the pointer. An italic serif title ("Lantern Festival") with a warm text glow floats above it all.


MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:

- Canvas lantern renderer: each lantern drawn with a radial additive glow halo, a gradient ellipse body, curved ribs, top/bottom caps, a knob and a hanging thread; lanterns rise, sway on sine paths and respawn from the bottom on a loop.
- Water reflections: per-lantern warm vertical gradient streaks plus wobbling horizontal shimmer dashes below a defined waterline for a rippling reflection.
- Parallax starfield: ~70-230 stars in two depth layers with twinkle phase, gold/white variation, occasional 4-point sparkle, and pointer-driven parallax shift.
- Ember sparks: additive warm dots spawned near the horizon that drift upward and fade, capped for performance.
- CSS moon with layered radial glow, a warm horizon glow behind the town, and a vignette for depth.
- Deterministic rooftop/town silhouette and rolling hills drawn as smooth quadratic paths.
- Responsive (clamp/vw/vh, resize) 320px->4K; prefers-reduced-motion pauses the animation while keeping a static composition.


INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:

- Move the pointer to shift the starfield and moon in parallax, giving the night a sense of depth you can "look around" in.
- Lanterns and embers rise and sway autonomously on continuous, non-repeating paths — the piece is a living, meditative loop.
- Click / tap to release an extra burst of lanterns and embers from the horizon.
- No navigation or chrome: a full-bleed, self-running night scene built to be admired, with motion respecting reduced-motion preferences.
