TITLE
-----
The Herbarium — A Cabinet of Pressed-Flower Specimens

DESIGN DESCRIPTION
------------------
An archival catalogue page for the fictional "Marlow Collection" (Catalogue No. 081), presenting eight pressed-flower specimens as a responsive grid of aged-paper index cards. Each card is a hand-monted sheet: a square "plate" window holds a flower built entirely from layered CSS petals (radially arranged, gradient-shaded, veined) around a pollen-dusted stamen, with a faded-ink specimen label beneath giving the italic binomial name, common name, Latin family, collector, date, locality and region, a hand-drawn rule, and a CSS barcode. The palette is sage green, cream and faded ink on a grainy paper background with a terracotta accent used for mounting tape, ribbon tabs and type highlights. Hovering (or tapping) a card lifts it and the flower "blooms" — petals rotate outward and scale up, saturation lifts, the stamen swells, and the card casts a deeper shadow — as if the pressed bloom were opening again. Cards rise into view on load with a staggered entrance.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES
-----------------------
- Pressed flowers built from pure CSS: petals are absolutely-positioned <span>s with border-radius petal shapes, layered gradients (to top, two-tone), inset shadows for volume, a central vein, and radial arrangement via CSS custom properties (--a angle, --spread bloom rotation, --i stagger index).
- Multi-layer bloom: petal layers offset by half a petal for a dense, natural rosette; hover/`.blooming` rotates petals outward and scales them, with per-petal transition-delay for a sequential opening.
- Archival paper aesthetic: repeating-linear-gradient grain overlay (multiply blend), soft inner vignette, aged-cream card gradients, mounting "tape" via ::before/::after, terracotta ribbon tabs and a CSS-drawn barcode.
- Faded-ink typography: Georgia serif for binomials and labels, wide-tracked sans caps for accession numbers and fact keys, italic family names.
- Signature effect: hover-to-bloom — a coordinated transform/saturation/stamen-scale animation that reopens each pressed flower.
- Responsive auto-fill card grid (minmax 252px) scaling 320px to 4K; staggered keyframe entrance rise; full prefers-reduced-motion fallback that disables bloom motion and shows flowers at rest.
- Accessible: cards are focusable <article role="button"> with Enter/Space to toggle bloom.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL
--------------------------
Passive browsing of an index: the user scrolls the specimen grid and hovers a card to make the pressed flower bloom and the card lift; on touch or keyboard they click/tap or press Enter/Space to toggle the bloom and read the archival label. Motion is celebratory but brief and degrades gracefully when reduced motion is requested. No navigation chrome — the whole page is the cabinet.
