TITLE:
Moonlight — "A Silver Night Over Still Water"

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A nocturne in silver and deep blue. A pale, luminous moon hangs in the upper third, wrapped in a wide pulsing halo that breathes slowly against the darkening gradient sky. Below the horizon a distant hill silhouette anchors the scene, and the lower half is a still lake that mirrors the moon in a broken, shimmering column of light. The water is alive but calm: fine horizontal silver waves roll with a slow sine motion, and the moon's reflection is rendered as stacked gradient streaks that wobble and fragment as if the surface is ever so slightly moving. Twinkling stars drift across the sky above, each on its own twinkle phase, while soft mist — gentle radial gradients — glides along the waterline and drifts off the edges. The palette stays strictly within silver, deep blue and the warm pale moon, so the whole page feels cool, quiet and luminous.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Radial-gradient moon with a layered halo (wide soft outer glow + tight inner bloom + shaded disc with faint craters) that pulses via a sine-driven radius.
- Animated water reflection: a vertical stack of short gradient streaks with per-row sine wobble, producing a shimmering broken moonpath.
- Gentle surface waves drawn as continuous sine polylines whose amplitude grows toward the foreground for perspective.
- Twinkling starfield: canvas dots on independent twinkle phases, with occasional four-point diffraction spikes on the brighter stars.
- Drifting mist layers as slowly parallaxed radial gradients that wrap around the screen edges.
- Pure <canvas> compositing of sky, stars, moon, hills, water and mist in a single requestAnimationFrame loop; DPR-aware for crisp 4K.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Calm and contemplative. Tap or click anywhere on the water to send an expanding elliptical ripple across the lake's surface (ripples flatten vertically to respect the water's perspective). Everything else — moon pulse, star twinkle, wave roll, mist drift — runs continuously and unattended, so the scene is a living, meditative backdrop.
