TITLE:
AFTER hours — Lo-Fi Vaporwave Sunset

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A single fixed viewport recreates a nostalgic synthwave dusk. A vertical sky gradient runs from deep indigo at the zenith through magenta and hot pink down to a warm amber at the horizon, where a chrome sun sits — a radial disc banded yellow→orange→hot-pink→purple with a soft double glow that pulses slowly, its lower half sliced by the classic horizontal slits (masked repeating lines that drift downward). Below the horizon a cyan-and-pink perspective grid floor is tilted into depth with rotateX and scrolled toward the viewer on a seamless loop, while a striped sun-reflection column shimmers on the "water." Hand-drawn SVG palm silhouettes frame both lower corners plus one small distant palm, twinkling stars flicker in the upper sky, and a drifting scanline overlay with an inset CRT vignette ties the retro aesthetic together. A monospace wordmark with layered pink/purple/cyan drop shadows flickers like a faulty neon sign.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- CSS perspective grid floor: a wide plane rotated with rotateX(74deg) and animated via seamless repeating-linear-gradient lines (vertical cyan + horizontal pink).
- Chrome gradient sun with masked repeating-linear-gradient "slits" on the lower half and a pulsing dual box-shadow glow.
- Hand-authored inline SVG palm-tree silhouettes (trunk + radiating frond paths) at three scales for foreground/midground depth.
- Layered atmospheric sky gradient + masked, striped sun-reflection column with a shimmer animation.
- CRT scanline overlay (repeating-linear-gradient, mix-blend-mode:overlay, slow drift) plus an inset vignette via box-shadow.
- Twinkling starfield built from multiple radial-gradient dots; flickering monospace title with multi-color text-shadow.
- Fully responsive sizing with clamp()/vw units; no external assets, no network requests.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Purely ambient and cinematic — the page is a moving loop, not a control surface. The grid scrolls forever, the sun breathes, scanlines drift and the title flickers on their own timers, so the viewer simply watches a perpetual synthwave dusk. There is no required interaction (no JS required); it is designed as a living wallpaper / idle visual.
