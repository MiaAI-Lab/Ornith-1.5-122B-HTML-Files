TITLE:
POP ROOM — The Bubble Wrap You Can Actually Pop

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
Staged as an editorial "Fidget Collection" plate, the page frames a full sheet of glossy pastel bubbles like a product photograph on soft grey. Each bubble is a layered radial-gradient sphere — a bright upper-left specular highlight, a lower-right bounce light, and a soft drop shadow — rendered in a rotating palette of sky blue, rose, mint, lilac and butter-yellow so no two rows read the same. Hold to squish (the sphere compresses vertically with a springy ease), release to pop: the bubble scales down with a bright flash while a six-point spark burst explodes outward and a tiny "+"/combo score floats up. A live mono readout tracks bubbles left, total popped and the current combo, and every pop fires a short WebAudio triangle-wave blip whose pitch climbs with the combo for a secretly musical, deeply satisfying sequence. Pop the whole sheet and it announces a fresh refill; a "Rain Mode" toggle pops every bubble in a cascading wave.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Glossy 3D bubble look built entirely from layered radial gradients (specular highlight + bounce light) plus inset and drop shadows on a 50%-radius span, no images.
- Press-to-squish using scaleY/scaleX on pointerdown with an overshoot cubic-bezier, releasing on pointerup.
- Pop animation via a keyframed scale-to-zero + brightness flash, then visibility:hidden for the refilled slot.
- Six-point radial spark burst using CSS custom-property --dx/--dy translation with per-particle angle and distance, plus a floating "+"/combo score label.
- WebAudio oscillator blips (triangle wave, exponential pitch ramp) whose base frequency rises with the combo count, created lazily and resumed on first gesture.
- Responsive grid that re-chooses column count (7→14) by viewport width and rebuilds on resize; haptic feedback via navigator.vibrate.
- Editorial masthead, live mono HUD, blurred pill controls and a multiply-blended ambient tint for a crafted, gallery feel.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Purely tactile and immediate — press and hold any bubble to squish it, let go to pop it (with sound, sparks, a floating score tick and a haptic buzz). Pop fast to build a combo that raises both your score multiplier and the blip pitch; the combo decays after ~0.9s of inactivity. The whole sheet auto-refills a moment after the last bubble pops, or the viewer can hit "Refill" at any time, toggle "Sound" on/off, or switch on "Rain Mode" so every bubble pops in a continuous cascading wave. Works with pointer, touch and keyboard (focus a bubble, press Enter/Space), and respects prefers-reduced-motion by disabling the burst and floating score.
