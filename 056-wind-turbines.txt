TITLE:

"Wind Farm" — clean energy on rolling sage hills under a soft sun.


DESIGN DESCRIPTION:

A calm, minimal landscape: a clean blue sky gradient fades down to a pale horizon with a soft, hazy sun glow in the upper corner. Soft white clouds — each built from a single dot plus layered box-shadow puffs — drift slowly across the sky on seamless loops. Three layers of rolling hills (a distant teal-sage, a mid sage, and a darker foreground sage) create depth, and six white wind turbines stand along the ridges. Each turbine is pure CSS — a tapered tower, a nacelle, a hub and three tapering blades — with its rotor tilted in perspective and spinning at its own speed (a couple reversing for realism). A subtle mouse parallax slides the hill layers, and a minimal typographic label anchors the composition.


MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:

- CSS wind turbines: tapered clip-path towers, nacelle, hub and three blade elements rotated at 0/120/240 degrees, each spinning on its own duration with a couple reversing direction.
- Perspective-rotating rotors: each rotor is wrapped in a perspective + rotateX transform so the blades sweep as a gentle ellipse, giving a believable 3D tilt.
- Clouds from one element: a small dot plus a multi-stop white box-shadow forms each fluffy cloud, drifted across the sky with a fade-in/fade-out loop.
- Layered inline SVG hills (far / mid / front) with sage-to-dark-green fills for depth, plus a soft radial sun glow and low haze band.
- Pointer parallax: the three hill layers translate at different rates with the cursor for a subtle 3D drift.
- Clean sans-serif type, a two-line label, and a restrained blue/sage/white palette for a serene, "clean energy" mood.
- Responsive (clamp/vw/vh, resize) 320px->4K; prefers-reduced-motion stops the cloud and turbine animations.


INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:

- Move the pointer to parallax the hill layers, giving the landscape a gentle sense of depth.
- Turbines rotate continuously at varied speeds (some reversed) and clouds drift on seamless loops — a serene, self-running scene.
- No controls or scrolling: a single full-bleed landscape built to be admired, with motion honoring reduced-motion preferences.
