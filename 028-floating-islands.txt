TITLE
-----
Aethermere — a sky-bound archipelago where waterfalls run back to the clouds.


DESIGN DESCRIPTION
------------------
A dreamlike high-altitude scene painted in sky blue, moss green, warm stone and a
soft violet haze near the horizon. A glowing distant sun sits high right, breathing
gently, casting a warm radial wash across a cool-to-warm gradient sky. Several
hand-built CSS islands drift at different depths — each a mossy grass cap crowned
with little round trees and, on the largest, a tiny cottage with a warm-lit window
— floating above jagged warm-stone undersides rendered with inset-shadow modelling
and an irregular clip-path. Their defining trick: instead of pouring down, the
waterfalls LEAP upward from a luminous source pool at each island's edge, rising
into the sky as an animated gradient stream (a scrolling highlight repeats
seamlessly) showered with rising, fading spray particles. Stacked cloud layers
built from soft SVG puffs drift and parallax at their own pace, violet haze bands
weave through the middle distance, and a small flock of hand-drawn birds glides
across the sky with a soft bob. Everything reacts to the pointer — moving or
dragging the mouse slides clouds, islands and haze at different depths while the
sun shifts — giving a true sense of floating inside a living diorama, finished
with a vignette, faint film grain and a serif caption.


MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES
-----------------------
- CSS-built islands: a rounded grass cap (asymmetric border-radius), a warm-stone
  body modelled with left-to-right + inset shadows, and an irregular rocky base cut
  with a polygon clip-path — no image assets, purely CSS.
- Soft, layered drop shadows (large blurred drop-shadow on each island and cloud)
  that sell the sense of weightless objects suspended in air.
- Signature upward waterfall: a vertical animated-gradient stream whose highlight
  band scrolls upward via a repeating-linear-gradient keyframe (seamless loop),
  combined with 10 procedurally animated spray particles that rise from a glowing
  source pool, drift sideways, fade in/out on a sine envelope and shrink — reversing
  gravity for the surreal effect.
- Depth-scaled mouse parallax: pointer (-1..1) eases toward target (lerp) and offsets
  clouds, haze and islands by their per-layer depth, near elements moving more than
  far ones.
- Gentle continuous motion everywhere: islands bob on offset sine waves, clouds and
  haze auto-drift, the sun breathes, birds bob and glide — alive even with no input.
- Soft-violet horizon haze via blurred radial-gradient bands for atmospheric
  perspective against the sky-blue-to-cream gradient.
- Inline-SVG clouds (clustered translucent ellipses) and inline-SVG bird strokes,
  all generated in JS; a glowing dual drop-shadow sun with a bright core disc.
- devicePixelRatio-independent vector graphics that rebuild on resize/orientation,
  keeping edges crisp from 320px to 4K; vignette + dotted grain overlay for mood.


INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL
--------------------------
- Move the mouse (or drag on touch) anywhere to look through the scene; clouds,
  haze, islands and the sun respond at their own depths for a layered parallax
  "float".
- The world also animates continuously on its own (bobbing islands, drifting clouds,
  rising waterfalls, gliding birds), so it is fully alive with no input; a tap nudges
  the viewpoint.
- A caption invites interaction and a center hint fades out on the first move.
- Fully responsive: all geometry (island positions, cloud spacing, waterfall sizes,
  bird field) is recomputed from the live viewport on resize and orientation change,
  and layers are rebuilt so nothing distorts or clips.
- No controls or menus — the interaction is purely embodied (drift through the sky),
  keeping the composition clean and gallery-like.
- Runs entirely offline with zero network requests; every visual is CSS / inline SVG
  / JS.
