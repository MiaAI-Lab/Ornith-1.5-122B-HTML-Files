TITLE:

"Terrarium at Dusk" — a warm glass jar garden with wandering fireflies.


DESIGN DESCRIPTION:

A single cozy glass jar sits on a wooden shelf against a deep night-blue gradient, its interior a miniature ecosystem: layered moss mounds, a little cactus, a fern, a round succulent and sprouting grasses built entirely from CSS shapes and gradients, with tiny pebbles and a crescent moon visible through the glass. Warm amber light glows from within while a soft condensation haze and a refraction rim trace the jar's curved edges. Behind and around the jar, canvas fireflies drift on slow wandering paths, each a soft additive glow that brightens and dims as it drifts, casting faint warmth onto the scene. The composition is a centered, intimate still-life with a vignette and gentle heat-haze, designed to feel warm, quiet and alive.


MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:

- Canvas firefly system: ~40 insects on perlin-ish wandering paths with per-firefly glow, twinkle phase, speed and a soft additive radial halo (globalCompositeOperation = 'lighter').
- CSS glass jar: translucent body via layered linear/radial gradients, curved refraction rim, bright specular highlights, a rim light and subtle chromatic edge.
- Layered CSS plants: moss mounds, cactus, fern, succulent and grasses built from clip-path, border-radius and gradient shading, stacked in back/mid/front parallax layers.
- Depth parallax: background, jar interior and foreground firefly layers translate at different rates from pointer movement.
- Ambient motion: condensation haze, a slow heat-haze distortion, drifting dust motes and a faint inner glow pulse for a "living" feel.
- Warm amber-on-deep-blue palette with a vignette and a small crescent moon for narrative detail.
- Responsive scaling (clamp/vw/vh, resize handler) from 320px to 4K; prefers-reduced-motion disables the animation loops.


INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:

- Move the pointer (or drag on touch) to parallax the scene and gently nudge the plants; the jar and foreground respond at different depths.
- Fireflies wander autonomously and never repeat exactly; they gather and scatter on their own slow rhythms.
- Click / tap a firefly (or the jar) to burst a warm glow and briefly attract nearby fireflies.
- No controls or UI chrome: the piece is a calm, self-running diorama meant to be admired, with motion honoring reduced-motion settings.
