TITLE
=====
THE GOLDEN HOUR — Autumn Forest

DESIGN DESCRIPTION
==================
A cinematic autumn scene built entirely on one canvas over a warm CSS sky
gradient. A low golden sun sits just above the horizon, wrapped in a large
additive glow and a fan of 46 shimmering volumetric rays whose opacity is
modulated by a gap pattern so they read as light leaking "through branches".
The forest is composed in receding layers for real depth: a hazy, warm-tinted
far canopy ridge, a mid ridge, and two large bare branching trees in the
foreground that frame the composition left and right. Between the layers,
roughly a hundred leaves fall and tumble — each a small gradient leaf silhouette
with a midrib, spinning and swaying on a per-leaf wind oscillation, drifting
faster and larger in the near plane than the far. Faint birds drift across the
upper sky. A warm atmospheric haze wash, a golden horizon bloom and an inset
vignette tie it together. Over it sits a serif italic wordmark ("The Golden
Hour") and a small mono meta readout of wind state and leaf count.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES
=======================
- Procedural bare trees via recursive branching (trunk splits into swept
  branches, tapering width/length, depth ~7), drawn every frame with a
  length-scaled sinusoidal sway so the canopy breathes in the wind.
- Layered parallax forest: far/mid canopy ridges built from filled circles
  along a wavy baseline plus a base fill; far ridge drawn lighter/warmer with a
  lighter-blend haze veil for atmospheric perspective.
- Volumetric sun rays: 46 additive triangular wedges radiating from the sun,
  each with a directional gradient, a per-ray "gap" value (light-through-
  branches) and a shimmer term; slow rotation.
- Radial-gradient sun with a hot white core and wide additive glow disc.
- Tumbling leaves: per-leaf position/rotation/sway phase, horizontal drift =
  global wind + sinusoidal sway, vertical fall scaled by depth; respawn at top
  when they leave the bottom for an endless drift.
- Depth sorting by draw order (far leaves behind mid ridge, near leaves in
  front) with depth-based alpha so near leaves are crisp and far leaves hazy.
- Wind model: eased target driven by cursor + slow sine + decaying "gust"
  envelope; clicks inject a gust that perturbs nearby leaves' spin/sway.
- CSS linear-gradient sky (crimson→amber→gold), screen-blend haze ::before,
  vignette ::after, custom screen-blend cursor, DPR-aware canvas.
- Editorial serif italic gradient wordmark + mono meta panel.
- prefers-reduced-motion freezes the animation loop; touch support included.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL
==========================
Ambient scene with gentle pointer interaction. Leaves fall, sway and tumble
forever; birds drift; the sun rays shimmer and the trees breathe on their own.
Moving the cursor bends the wind (left/right changes the drift direction and
strength, reflected in the live "wind" readout). Clicking / tapping sends a
gust through the canopy — a burst of wind that flings nearby leaves and adds
spin. A custom sunlit cursor follows the pointer on fine-pointing devices and
hides on touch. Fully responsive, offline, and motion-sensitive friendly
(animation halts under prefers-reduced-motion).
