TITLE:
折り鶴 · Orizuru — The Folded Crane

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A single serene stage holds a sheet of washi paper under a slow, tilted 3D camera that gently sways. The crane assembles itself through a choreographed sequence of CSS 3D panel folds: a vermilion diamond body lifts first, then the two cream wings swing up and out on a central spine, the indigo neck rises and its head kinks, and finally the tail sweeps back — each fold staggered so the paper appears to fold itself in mid-air. Every panel is double-sided (vermilion/cream/indigo front and cream/vermilion back via backface-visibility) so the reverse of each crease reads as a different paper tone, with soft blurred ground shadows that breathe as the model lifts. A faint washi fiber texture overlays the whole page, a vertical kanji caption and a small romaji wordmark keep the composition minimal, and a FOLD / UNFOLD control lets you freeze the crane flat or let it fold again.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- CSS 3D folding panels: absolutely-positioned divs with clip-path polygon shapes, transform-style:preserve-3d and rotateY/translateZ folds about per-panel transform-origins.
- Sequential fold animation: a shared 6.4s timeline with staggered keyframe starts (body → wings → neck → head → tail) for a self-folding effect, reversible via a paused class.
- Two-sided "paper": front/back face elements with backface-visibility:hidden, each face a different palette tone so reverse creases look authentic.
- Washi texture built purely from layered repeating-linear-gradients (fine fibers) at low opacity over a cream radial page.
- Soft drop shadows: a blurred radial ground shadow that scales/fades ("breathes") as the crane lifts, plus gentle translateZ lift on the body.
- A slow perspective sway on the camera (rotateX/rotateZ keyframes) and a vertical-rl kanji caption for Japanese minimalist framing.
- Zero external assets; fully responsive via clamp()/min()/vw sizing.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
The crane auto-plays a fold cycle on load. A single FOLD / UNFOLD button toggles a paused state that freezes the model flat on the paper (unfolded) or resumes the fold — letting the viewer pause the transformation at the flat sheet or watch it assemble. Beyond that toggle the experience is a gentle ambient loop (the camera sways continuously), so it reads as a meditative, slow-moving object rather than a fast interaction.
