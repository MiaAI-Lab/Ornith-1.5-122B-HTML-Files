TITLE:
Frost — Twilight Clock

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A single frosted-glass panel floats over a slowly breathing twilight sky. The background is a conic indigo→violet→teal gradient that drifts on a 30-second cycle, with three large, heavily-blurred orbs (teal, rose, lilac) drifting on independent paths and screen-blending behind the glass. The panel itself uses backdrop-filter blur + saturation with a bright inner highlight and soft outer shadow so it reads as real frosted acrylic. Inside, an analog dial with glowing cyan numerals (the cardinal 12/3/6/9 switch to a rose glow and grow slightly larger) and 60 fine tick marks sits above a monospaced digital readout; a thin rose→teal second hand sweeps continuously with a counterweight tail, while the hour and minute hands move smoothly. Below the clock the current local time, full date, and resolved timezone abbreviation are shown in tabular, softly glowing digits.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(1.5) on the card and a lighter blur on the dial for genuine frost.
- Animated conic-gradient sky (background-position keyframes over a 300%-wide gradient) plus three screen-blended, 50px-blurred drifting orbs.
- Analog clock built from positioned hand divs with transform-origin: 50% 100%, rotated by JS; continuous sweep via requestAnimationFrame using milliseconds.
- Glowing numerals and ticks generated by JS using trig (cos/sin) and placed with percentage left/top around the dial; text-shadow glow in cyan, cardinals in rose.
- Tabular-nums monospace digital readout with glow; live timezone resolution via Intl.DateTimeFormat.
- prefers-reduced-motion: freezes background/orb motion and snaps the clock to a per-second tick.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Passive, ambient — the page is a live wall clock. Nothing is clickable; the user simply reads the time as hands sweep and the twilight sky drifts behind the frost. Motion and cadence adapt automatically to the OS reduced-motion setting.
