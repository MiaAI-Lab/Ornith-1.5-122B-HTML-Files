TITLE:
MANDALA — Still the Center

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A square, perfectly symmetric mandala floats in the center of a deep indigo void, breathing slowly as if alive. Nine concentric rings of motifs — beads, teardrop petals, kites, radiating rays, fine ticks and an outer ring of hairline strokes — are generated with exact N-fold radial symmetry and spun at different speeds, with adjacent rings counter-rotating so the pattern perpetually weaves against itself. A soft gold-and-rose aura glows behind it, faint guide circles trace the rings, and a scattered starfield twinks at random. At the heart sits a central lotus of eight petals around a radiant gold core, the still point around which the whole field turns. Serif wordmarks — "Still the Center" above, "breathe in · and out" below — frame it as a meditative object rather than a decoration.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Single inline <svg viewBox="0 0 1000 1000"> in a 1/1 aspect-ratio frame that scales fluidly from 320px to 4K with no external assets.
- Radial symmetry built procedurally in JS: each ring is N copies of a motif placed by rotate(angle) + translate(0,-radius), so the geometry is mathematically exact and lightweight.
- Counter-rotation via two CSS animation classes (.rL / .rR) whose duration is set per-ring with a CSS custom property (--d), giving each ring its own slow, opposing period.
- A breathing wrapper group scales 1 → 1.04 on a 10s ease-in-out loop; a radial-gradient aura pulses behind the whole field.
- Motifs drawn as hand-authored SVG paths/polygons (teardrop petals, kite diamonds, ray lines with tip beads) in a curated indigo / gold / rose palette, plus a radial-gradient glowing core.
- A gentle pointer-driven drift on the background aura (smoothed with requestAnimationFrame easing), and a prefers-reduced-motion branch that stops every animation.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Essentially contemplative and self-playing — the mandala rotates, counter-weaves and breathes on its own, inviting the viewer to pause. There are no buttons or controls; the only input is subtle — moving the pointer (or dragging on touch) gently nudges the luminous aura so it seems to follow the eye, like light shifting across a surface. When left alone, the piece performs its slow, endless, hypnotic rotation indefinitely. On prefers-reduced-motion, all rotation, breathing, glow and twinkle stop, leaving a fully symmetric, static mandala that still reads as a complete composition.
