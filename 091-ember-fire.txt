TITLE:
Ember Fire — Rising Sparks

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A campfire glows low and hot against a near-black night. A bed of coals is rendered on a full-bleed canvas as a field of pulsing radial-gradient embers, each breathing at its own tempo so the whole bed shimmers like real coals. Above them a soft, blurred flame of layered 'lighter'-composited blobs flickers with layered sine noise. Hundreds of embers rise from the bed — gentle ones drift and cool from white-hot to deep orange, while brighter sparks launch upward and arc back under gravity. A translucent heat-haze band above the fire is distorted in real time by an SVG fractal-noise displacement filter, giving the signature shimmer. A warm light pool and a heavy vignette wrap the scene in intimate, moody light.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Full-bleed <canvas> particle system with per-frame trail fade for silky motion
- Two particle behaviours in one pool: drifting embers and gravity-arc sparks, cooling red->orange as they age (radial-gradient glow + hot core)
- Coal bed built from pulsing, per-coal radial gradients scaled into ellipses
- Gentle flame from layered 'lighter'-blended teardrop radial gradients driven by multi-sine flicker
- Heat-haze distortion via an inline SVG feTurbulence + feDisplacementMap filter on a gradient band
- Warmth light-pool (mix-blend-mode: screen) and a moody radial vignette
- DPR-aware resize handling; pointer stoking and click/tap spark bursts

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Ambient by default — coals pulse and embers/sparks rise continuously. Moving the pointer over the scene "stokes" the fire, spawning extra embers and brightening the flames; clicking or tapping at a point launches a burst of sparks from that spot. Motion is gracefully reduced under prefers-reduced-motion.
