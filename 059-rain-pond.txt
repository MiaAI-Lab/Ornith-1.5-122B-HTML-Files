TITLE:
THE POND — Rain on Still Water

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A low grey sky over dark water, rendered as a single moody canvas. Diagonal rain stitches down in translucent streaks while the surface breathes: ripples — each a set of nested elliptical rings — spawn at random across the pond and bloom outward, fading as they widen, and a pale reflection column shivers and drifts on the water with a slow, living shimmer. Click (or tap) anywhere to drop a stone: a larger ripple opens with a little crown of splash droplets and a soft sonar blip. The whole scene is staged as an editorial "Weather Room" plate — a serif masthead, a live mono HUD tracking rain, ripples and frame rate, and glassy pill controls for intensity, sound and wind.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas ripple rings: nested ellipses (squashed vertically for perspective) that grow and fade, drawn with additive-feeling translucent strokes and a small rising splash-crown of droplets.
- Diagonal rain streaks as short line segments advected by a configurable wind vector, recycled as they fall off the bottom.
- Faint reflection shimmer built from an animated radial light column plus six horizontal "light band" gradients whose alpha and width oscillate with sine functions of time.
- DPR-aware rendering (canvas.width = logicalW * dpr, ctx.setTransform scale) so it stays crisp from 320px to 4K, with a resize rebuild of the drop field.
- WebAudio: a looping filtered white-noise bed (bandpass + lowpass) for ambient rain whose gain tracks intensity, and a per-drop sine blip with an exponential pitch fall.
- Three intensities (Calm / Steady / Downpour) and a Wind toggle that reseed the rain field and retune the ambience; auto-spawned ripples keep the surface alive.
- Editorial masthead, live mono HUD, glassy backdrop-blur chips and a multiply mood for a crafted, gallery feel.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Ambient and tactile. The pond rains and ripples on its own, but the viewer is invited to "drop a stone" — click or tap anywhere on the water to open a large ripple with a splash and a blip. Controls let them dial the rain from Calm to Downpour (which also brightens the ambience and raises the ripple cadence), toggle the sound on/off, and flip Wind on to bend the rain diagonal and make it patter sideways. A hint fades away once the first ripple is made. Pointer, mouse and touch all work, and prefers-reduced-motion disables the splash droplets and hint.
