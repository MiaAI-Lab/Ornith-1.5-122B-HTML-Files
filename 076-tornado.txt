TITLE
=====
MAELSTROM — Storm Cell 076

DESIGN DESCRIPTION
==================
A cinematic supercell scene rendered entirely on one canvas over a stormy CSS
backdrop. A full-height funnel dominates the frame: its body is a solid,
rippling cone built each frame from a sampled left/right outline whose radius
tapers from a wide dust-choked base to a narrow neck, with a dark inner core
for depth and a screen-blended lit streak that slides across the wall so the
funnel reads as rotating. A real vortex particle field — six hundred+ motes —
spirals upward inside it: each particle has an angle, a height and a radial
position, orbits faster near the neck, rises on an updraft, wobbles with
turbulence, and respawns at the base when it reaches the top. Forty-plus debris
chunks (tumbling dark squares) whirl through the lower third, and a band of
soft radial dust clouds rolls out along the wet ground. Angled rain streaks
sweep across on the wind, an ominous horizon glow sits behind the storm, and
random lightning flashes wash the whole scene pale blue-white and brighten the
particles. A soft vignette and an editorial wordmark (serif italic
"Maelstrom") plus a mono storm-telemetry panel (wind, lean, debris, state, gust
meter) complete the composition.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES
=======================
- Rippling funnel body: left/right outline sampled at 30 heights, radius =
  geometric taper × perspective narrowing + layered sinusoidal turbulence;
  filled with a vertical gradient, a dark inner core cone and a screen-blend
  lit streak that drifts horizontally to sell rotation.
- Vortex particle model: per-particle angle/height/radius, angular speed that
  increases toward the neck, a per-particle updraft, turbulence wobble, alpha
  and size falloff with height, warm-tinted dust motes mixed in; sorted
  far-first for correct overlap.
- Debris system: larger, darker, faster-tumbling chunks confined to the lower
  funnel, each rotating independently and respawning from the base.
- Ground dust: a field of soft radial-gradient clouds rolling outward along the
  wet ground with a lean-driven drift; wet-ground specular highlight strip.
- Steering: pointer position drives a top-heavy "lean" (funnel bends toward the
  pointer, eased); pointer movement and clicks inject a decaying "gust" that
  speeds the spin, kicks debris and widens the lit streak.
- Lightning: randomized flash timer drives a full-screen radial screen-blend
  flash and multiplies particle brightness, with rapid exponential decay.
- Perspective funnel geometry: radius narrows and vertical position compresses
  with height (pow 0.72) for a convincing 3D cone on a 2D canvas.
- Storm atmosphere: CSS radial/linear storm gradients, screen-blend horizon
  glow, angled canvas rain, and a radial vignette.
- DPR-aware canvas, responsive re-layout, live telemetry readout + gust meter.
- prefers-reduced-motion draws a single still frame and halts the loop.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL
==========================
A steerable storm. Moving the pointer left/right bends the funnel toward it
(the live "lean" readout tracks it) and the whole vortex leans and shifts; the
faster the pointer moves, the more "gust" builds, spinning the particles up,
whipping the debris and flaring the lit streak. Clicking / tapping delivers a
sharp gust and a lean toward the cursor. A gust meter and a state readout
("steady" → "gust" → "violent") respond in real time; Space re-ignites motion
under reduced-motion. Angled rain and drifting ground dust reinforce the wind
direction. Fully responsive, offline, and motion-sensitive friendly (the
animation halts under prefers-reduced-motion, leaving a single rendered frame).
