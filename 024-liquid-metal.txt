RECREATION PROMPT
=================
Create a single self-contained HTML file (inline <style> plus inline <script>,
no external assets) of a dark, product-style hero showing a morphing "liquid
chrome" blob. Center a large circular form on a dark radial-gradient stage with
a faint reflective environment glow and a soft perspective "reflection pool"
beneath it. The blob is a div with a multi-stop diagonal linear-gradient in
silver/chrome tones, an animated morphing multi-value border-radius (the
signature effect), a slow hue-rotate loop, and layered sheen/sweep/specular
highlight pseudo-elements and child divs (screen blend). Behind it, a full-size
<canvas> continuously draws flowing horizontal chrome light bands (sine-moved
vertical gradients) and a few drifting cyan radial highlight points. The blob
wrapper reacts to the pointer: translate + rotateX/rotateY tilt with a
smoothed (lerped) follow. Overlay minimal chrome-tinted type — a top brand/status
row and a bottom headline with a metallic gradient-text fill, a caption and an
interaction hint. Use system sans-serif. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

TITLE
=====
MERCURO · Liquid Chrome

DESIGN DESCRIPTION
==================
A dark, premium product hero for a fictional "liquid metal" element. Against a
near-black blue-grey radial-gradient stage, a large chrome sphere/blob sits
center, its surface a convincing polished-metal look built from a multi-stop
diagonal gradient (bright white highlight → silver → steel grey → dark) plus
inset and drop shadows. Its defining motion is a continuously morphing border-
radius that oozes between organic shapes (a 12s loop), combined with a slow
24s hue-rotate so the metal subtly shifts tone. On top of the base form, several
light layers add realism: a rotating conic sheen, a horizontal specular sweep,
and two bobbing specular hot spots, all screen-blended. Beneath the blob, a
blurred perspective "reflection pool" pulses gently. A full-screen canvas
behind everything draws slow-flowing horizontal ribbons of light and drifting
cyan glows, suggesting a moving reflective environment. Typography is minimal
and metallic: a spaced brand with a glowing wordmark, a live status readout,
and a large ultra-light headline "Liquid Metal" filled with a silver gradient
(background-clip: text). The caption explains that moving the pointer steers
the reflection.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES
=======================
- Signature morphing blob: multi-value border-radius animated across five
  keyframes (morph, 12s) plus a continuous hue-rotate loop (hue, 24s).
- Chrome material: multi-stop diagonal linear-gradient (white → silver → steel
  → dark) with inset highlight + deep drop shadows for a polished look.
- Layered light on the form: rotating conic-gradient sheen (spin, screen),
  translating linear specular sweep (sweep, screen), and two bobbing radial
  specular hot spots (bob).
- Canvas background: requestAnimationFrame loop drawing 6 sine-oscillating
  horizontal light bands (createLinearGradient fills) and 3 drifting cyan
  radial highlight points; DPR-aware resize.
- Pointer-reactive 3D tilt: pointermove/touchmove set targets, a rAF loop
  eases (lerps at 0.06) toward them and applies translate3d + rotateX/rotateY
  on the blob wrapper for a parallax "steer the reflection" feel.
- Reflection pool: perspective rotateX ellipse with a pulsing opacity/scale
  (reflectPulse) and blur.
- Metallic gradient text headline via linear-gradient + background-clip: text,
  color: transparent.
- Ambient environment glows (layered radial gradients) and a soft pulse dot.
- Dark chrome palette via CSS custom properties (--chrome-hi/1/2/3/lo, --accent
  cyan).
- prefers-reduced-motion disables all animations.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL
==========================
Pointer-driven and ambient. The blob continuously morphs and shifts hue on its
own, and the canvas environment flows automatically. The core interaction is
the pointer: moving the mouse (or dragging with a touch) across the viewport
tilts the blob in 3D (rotateX/rotateY) with a slight translate parallax, using
a smoothed lerp so the motion feels fluid and "liquid" — as if steering the
reflection across its surface. No clicks or scrolling drive the effect; it is a
demonstration piece meant to be hovered. Under prefers-reduced-motion all
animation (morph, hue, sheen, sweep, canvas, tilt loop effects) is disabled.
