TITLE: Clay — Mood Selector

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A soft, pillowy "How are you feeling?" mood board rendered entirely in claymorphism. A rounded pastel card floats on a warm off-white backdrop, its surface extruded with dual inner/outer shadows so every element looks molded from soft clay. Five blobby mood chips (Great, Good, Okay, Tired, Awful), each a different pastel, sit in a responsive grid; clicking one squishes it inward, lifts it into a glowing ring, and updates a friendly status line plus an empathetic little message. Butter-yellow, mint, peach and lavender accents keep the palette gentle and tactile, with a soft gradient glow behind the card giving it depth.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Clay extrusion: layered double drop-shadows (a light top-left outer + a dark bottom-right outer) combined with inset highlights and shadows to fake soft 3D clay.
- Squish interaction: `transform: scale()` + reduced shadow on :active and a springy hover lift, using cubic-bezier easing for a tactile "press".
- Selected-state glow: an inner ring + colored outer halo on the active mood chip.
- Fully CSS-drawn blobby chips (border-radius, no images) with pastel gradient fills.
- Warm ambient gradient glow + soft page background for depth.
- Responsive grid (auto-fit minmax) and fluid clamp() typography; prefers-reduced-motion support.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Single-screen tap/click app. The user selects exactly one mood by clicking a chip; the chosen chip squishes and highlights while the others relax, and the message area animates to a context-specific response. A "reset" affordance (re-clicking or a clear button) returns to the neutral state. No navigation, no scroll — a focused, delightful micro-interaction.
