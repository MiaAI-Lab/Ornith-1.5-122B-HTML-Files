TITLE:
Grand Soirée du Siècle — 1925 Deco Poster

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A 1920s event poster framed like a gilded jewel. A deep emerald radial ground sits inside a multi-layered gold-and-black keyline border with stepped ziggurat corners — the signature Deco motif — at all four edges. Above the title, a 48-ray sunburst (generated in SVG) rotates imperceptibly behind a soft golden core, its long and short rays alternating like a rising fan. The headline is set in heavy, wide-tracked serif caps stacked in three tiers, filled with a top-lit gold gradient that reads as polished brass. A symmetric chevron-and-diamond rule underlines the title, followed by French cabaret-style small caps and a gold gradient rule capped with a lozenge. A circular medallion bearing "19 / 25" anchors the base. A diagonal light shimmer sweeps the whole poster on a slow loop, so the gold appears to catch the light.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Layered box-shadow "frame stack" (alternating gold-deep / ink / gold-lo bands) to build the stepped Deco border with no images.
- Stepped ziggurat corners assembled from six thin gold bars per corner, mirrored with scaleX/scaleY for the other three corners.
- 48 alternating long/short sunburst rays generated in JS and drawn as SVG paths radiating from center, slowly rotating.
- Gold gradient text (background-clip:text) on the headline for a brushed-metal look.
- Symmetric hand-built SVG chevron rule (mirrored point lists) with a center diamond.
- A screen-blended diagonal shimmer gradient animating across the poster for a metallic light-catch.
- Subtle pointer-driven parallax tilt (rotateX/Y) on the poster for depth; full prefers-reduced-motion fallback.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Largely static and elegant, with quiet life: the sunburst turns slowly, the gold shimmer drifts, and hovering the poster tilts it gently in 3D toward the pointer (pointermove), resetting on leave. All motion honors prefers-reduced-motion. On mobile it's a tap-to-look, no controls needed — the poster simply presents itself and stays crisp from 320px to 4K via clamp() sizing and aspect-ratio.
