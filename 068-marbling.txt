TITLE:
Ebru — Paper Marbling Reveal

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A study of the Ebru art of marbling: vivid crimson, gold, teal and cobalt pigment floats on a viscous cream "size," combed into concentric veins and swirling blooms that slowly drift and morph on canvas. The pattern is hidden beneath a sheet of cream paper; on lifting the sheet, the marbled skin blooms into view as the pigment veins grow from latent to fully formed, then continues to evolve gently — the single gesture that catches the fluid pattern forever. A subtle paper-fibre texture, multiply-blended pigment overlap and a soft size-sheen complete the tactile feel.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas "fluid" marbling: layered sine-wave combed veins + drifting radial pigment washes that morph over time.
- A bloom factor that grows the veins/washes from 0 → 1 on reveal (the pattern "blooms into" existence).
- Multiply blend for the pigment so overlapping colors deepen like real pigment on size.
- Fine comb texture (many thin wavy lines) over broad veins for authentic Ebru comb marks.
- A CSS paper "lift sheet" with fibre speckle, fold shadow and a 3D-ish lift-up reveal transition.
- Gradient cream bath + radial size-sheen highlight for a wet, tactile surface.
- Regenerates a fresh random pattern on "Re-submerge"; system fonts only, fully inline.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
The marbled pattern forms beneath a paper sheet. Click "Lift the sheet" (or tap the sheet) to peel the paper away and reveal the marbling as it blooms into place. "Re-submerge" lays a fresh sheet down, generates a new random pattern, and lifts again after the paper settles. Motion is ambient and gentle; prefers-reduced-motion minimizes the drift.
