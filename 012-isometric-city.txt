TITLE: Little City, Full Day — an isometric metropolis that lives through a whole day

DESIGN DESCRIPTION:
A tiny isometric city sits on a warm plaza and quietly lives out one full day. A seeded layout scatters dozens of low-poly buildings — each a real 3D box built from five CSS planes (front, back, left, right walls plus a rooftop) — across a grid of streets, with a central plaza left open. The sky behind the city is a single smooth gradient that the page re-derives every frame, walking from the pale rose of dawn, through a bright hazy noon, into a deep violet dusk and a starry indigo night, then looping. As the light falls, the tiny windows on every wall flicker on one by one with staggered warm glows, rooftops sprout blinking red beacons, and cars — small glowing dots — stream along the streets. Typography is quiet and editorial: a small caption and a live clock reading the current time and phase. The whole scene is a self-contained, endlessly looping diorama.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- True CSS 3D buildings: each is a footprint in the x-y plane with height along +z, walls hinged at their ground edges and rotated into place with rotateX/rotateY and precise transform-origins (verified against the rotation matrices).
- Isometric camera: a single rotateX(60deg) rotateZ(45deg) on the world, with a perspective container, gives the classic iso tilt.
- A day-night gradient is computed in JS each frame from a normalized time-of-day (0–1) by interpolating five key color stops (dawn / morning / noon / dusk / night) for sky, ground and ambient light.
- Animated window lights: each vertical wall carries a window grid — a static glass reflection layer (repeating-linear-gradient) plus a masked warm-glow layer that fades in and out with a per-window staggered animation delay, so the city ignites unevenly.
- Blinking rooftop beacons (a thin CSS pole with a pulsing red dot) on taller buildings.
- Traffic: small glowing "car" dots driven along fixed lanes by a JS rAF loop (translate3d), with a subtle headlight glow.
- Stars that fade in only after dusk, plus a soft vignette and a moving highlight on the water.
- Seeded PRNG (mulberry32) for a reproducible, natural-looking building layout and randomized window timings.
- Fully responsive: the scene scales with the viewport; all sizes use relative units and the camera distance is tuned for both phone and 4K.

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
- Passive to watch, gentle to play with. The city runs itself — a continuous day-night loop with living windows and traffic — meant to sit on screen as ambient motion.
- Pointer drag (or swipe) rotates the whole world around the vertical axis for a full 360° view, with momentum that eases to a stop when released.
- A small on-screen clock shows the current time-of-day and phase (Dawn / Morning / Noon / Dusk / Night), reinforcing the loop.
- No buttons or menus: the experience is a contemplative, auto-playing diorama you can pick up and turn.
