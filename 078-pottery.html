Wet clay between the palms, the wheel humming beneath. Centrifugal force lifts the mound into a wall; the thumbs find the centre and the vessel breathes outward. Drag across the clay to set the wheel spinning, or up and down to pull its belly full or thin.

Drag sideways to spin · drag up/down to shape
